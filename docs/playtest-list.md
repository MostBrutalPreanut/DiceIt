# Human playtest list

What the bots cannot judge (from the balance changes plan, phase 8.2). The research players (`Human` in
`tools/research/lib.ts`) flip with human-like misses and reaction times, but they never nudge, never aim a shot and never
learn a table. Each item below says what to try and what to watch for. Opt-in data from players (the feedback form,
`docs/feedback.md`) is the other half of this list: a note on any of these is worth more than another bot run.

| What | Try | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **Crossed Wires** (boss) | Play the boss with a mid table, once without warning and once knowing it is coming | Bots swap hands perfectly (no effect); a bot that presses the old hand 30% of the time drops to a 0-11% pass rate. Where between is a person? |
| **Goal!** | Place it high on the table and aim for it | Can a person shoot past the goalie on purpose? The bots only hit it by chance. Does the Cook's order on it (now used up like any other part) read clearly? |
| **Duck Gallery** | Place it on a clear shot line | Is the speed-up fun or just frustrating after a few hits? |
| **Rubber Feet** | Nudge a ball that is heading for the drain | Is the extra shove noticeable? Nothing in the bots nudges. |
| **The run-out and closing time** | Let the ball clock run out with a busy table (several catchers, a Bucket, Kickbacks) | Do the bumpers going dead, the catchers letting go and the lights going out part by part read as "closing time"? Does Big Boi's gulp (the cash-in) at 15 s ever happen, and is it clear what it paid? |
| **One clock per ball** | Twin Ball, a Ball Printer, a Gumball lock | Is it clear that extra balls share the launched ball's clock (the HUD clock never resets for them)? |
| **The new Helping Hand** | Place one facing left and one facing right (its Faces dial) | Is a small flipper on the ← or → button useful for a weak player, or just confusing? It is now $2. |
| **Hourglass** | Shoot it from a flipper | Does +1 s a flipper shot feel worth aiming for? Is the "+1 S" popup readable? |
| **Night Shift** | Play it with a lights build | Does "the board darkens, the lights pay half" read? |
| **The Jammer** | Play it with one part that carries the table | It now jams the single best part of the last round (never Big Boi). Is that a fair boss or a run-ender? |
| **Money** | A whole run | With $4 a cleared round and $1 a ball left, does every purchase feel like a decision, or does the shop feel stingy? |

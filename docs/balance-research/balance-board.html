Big Boi Balance Board

Everything the balance research found, one decision at a time. Open an item, read what we saw and why, pick a fix (one is marked as recommended), set its status and leave a note. Your choices are saved as you go, and Claude can read them back to make the changes.

Status, 26 Oct: every item is decided. The final decisions are turned into a step-by-step plan in the repo, docs/balance-changes-plan.md, to be built in another session.

The goal every fix is judged against

  • Round goals are round numbers that are easy to remember: about 1,000 in round 1, about 2.5–3 million in round 15.
  • Great builds are possible, with luck and skill. A great build can win big, and then be tested in endless mode.
  • Losing runs and clutch runs happen too. Most good runs should pass narrowly; sometimes wildly; sometimes not.
  • A medium or low skill player who finishes the game clutch with a good build is a good result. A bad build should lose, even with great hands.
